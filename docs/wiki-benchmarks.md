## Combined design

New players first need the meaning of DMZ MW4, release/access status and a clear next action. Returning players need a direct route to locations, weapons, mission types and extraction. Use navigable task cards and searchable/filterable references rather than stacking every category on the homepage.

A clickable location schematic teaches geography but cannot replace a validated in-game map: state this before route planning. Weapon cards can show confirmed names/acquisition while excluding invented performance numbers. Mission pages can explain verified systems, with objective walkthroughs added after reliable evidence exists.

Shared navigation must support hover, touch, keyboard and Escape. Filters need visible state and no-result feedback. Preserve view state across tabs. Provide initial-HTML body content and standard links. These are requirements for this project, not claims about reference-site behavior.

Homepage targets DMZ MW4 / MW4 DMZ. Maps, weapons, missions, extraction and access each address a distinct need. Localized counterparts preserve the topic and switch to the same current page. Keep source review dates meaningful. Do not advertise comparative completeness, superior update speed or tested gameplay without evidence.
